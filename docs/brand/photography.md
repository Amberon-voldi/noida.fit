# Photography & Visual Direction — NOIDA.FIT

## 1. Core Visual Philosophy

Visual imagery on NOIDA.FIT must look and feel like **real human life unfolding across Noida**.

We capture the authentic texture of our city: morning mist rising over the synthetic track at Noida Stadium, the golden reflection of sunrise against the glass facades of the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway, breathless group laughter after a tough interval set, and the post-run chai circle at a local roadside tea stall.

---

## 2. Visual Principles

### 1. Real People, Real Diversity, Real Bodies
- Feature everyday runners, lifters, cyclists, and weekend athletes of diverse ages and fitness levels.
- Avoid hyper-sculpted, airbrushed bodybuilders or sterile fashion models wearing immaculate, uncreased gear.
- Celebrate sweat, effort, smiles, concentration, and camaraderie.

### 2. Grounded in Noida Geography
- Every image should feel distinctly rooted in Noida or Greater Noida:
  - **The Tracks:** Red clay and synthetic tracks of Noida Stadium (Sector 21A).
  - **The Corridors:** Wide service lanes along the Expressway (Sectors 93A, 128, 137, 142).
  - **The Greenery:** Dense tree lines of Meghdootam Park, Biodiversity Park, and City Park Greater Noida.
  - **The Urban Landmarks:** Advant Navis, Pari Chowk, Sector 18 underpasses, DLF Mall of India precinct.

### 3. Natural Lighting & Golden Hours
- Most real grassroots fitness in India happens between **5:15 AM and 7:45 AM**, or in the evening under stadium floodlights.
- Photography should capture early dawn light, warm sunrise glows, and deep evening stadium blues.
- Avoid harsh midday overhead sun or clinical white studio flash photography.

### 4. Group Energy & Candid Moments
- Prioritize photos of 3 to 30 people moving together, stretching together, or walking side-by-side.
- Prefer candid, unposed action (running mid-stride, passing water bottles, fist-bumping at the finish line) over stiff lineup group poses staring at the lens.

## 3. Image Categories & Aspect Ratios

| Asset Type | Primary Aspect Ratio | Minimum Pixel Dimensions | Focal Composition Guidance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Homepage Hero** | `16:9` or `21:9` | `1920x1080` (Desktop), `1080x1350` (Mobile) | Wide panoramic group shot moving toward or past the camera; leaves negative space for hero headline. |
| **Community Banner** | `16:9` or `3:1` | `1200x400` | Wide shot of the community together on their home turf. |
| **Event Cover** | `16:9` | `1200x675` | Action shot specific to the activity (e.g. runners at sunrise, outdoor kettlebell circle). |
| **Place / Venue** | `4:3` or `16:9` | `800x600` | Clear view of the track, court, or facility entrance in good ambient light. |
| **Editorial Story** | `3:2` | `1200x800` | Editorial documentary-style framing (portrait of a captain, detailed shot of running shoes). |
| **Community Avatar**| `1:1` Square | `400x400` | Distinct club crest, team logo, or clear leader portrait. |

---

## 4. What We Strictly Avoid

- ❌ **Generic Stock Models:** White studio backgrounds with models holding spotless 2kg pink dumbbells.
- ❌ **Aggressive Bodybuilding Tropes:** Ripped, oil-sheened bodybuilders scowling at mirrors in dark warehouse basements.
- ❌ **Western Suburban Stereotypes:** Empty American suburban sidewalks, coastal beaches, or mountain pines that bear zero resemblance to Northern India.
- ❌ **Over-Filtered Gradients:** Heavy Instagram vignettes or hyper-saturated HDR filters that make skin tones look artificial.

---

## 5. Guidance for Future AI Agents & Image Generation

When generating demo assets or mock placeholders for NOIDA.FIT:
1. **Always use specific Noida environmental prompts:** Include terms like *"morning run in Noida Expressway green belt"*, *"sunrise at synthetic track in Noida Stadium"*, *"active Indian runners and cyclists group"*, *"candid post-run tea stall gathering in urban India"*.
2. **Never leave broken image links:** If external demo photos are unavailable, use clean SVG placeholders styled with the NOIDA.FIT brand palette (`--surface`, `--border-subtle`, with an energetic accent icon) rather than broken `<img>` tags.
