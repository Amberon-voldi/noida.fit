# Brand Guidelines — NOIDA.FIT

## 1. Brand Ethos & Personality

NOIDA.FIT sits at the exact intersection of **City Culture, Grassroots Fitness, Social Community, and Modern Technology**.

We are not an intimidating bodybuilding gym, an expensive corporate club, or an impersonal software dashboard. We are the cultural heartbeat of an ambitious, energetic Indian city waking up at 5:30 AM to run, ride, lift, and move together.

### Personality Pillars

| Trait | How It Shows Up in the Product | What We Avoid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Urban & Modern** | Clean architectural typography, bold grid layouts, sharp contrast, subtle location coordinates. | Rustic, vintage, or generic suburban gym aesthetics. |
| **Energetic & Active** | Dynamic visual rhythm, action photography, electric accent colors used intentionally. | Sluggish layouts, washed-out pastels, hyper-saturated neon chaos. |
| **Social & Community-Driven** | Interfaces spotlight group photos, captain names, meeting points, and shared rituals. | Solitary athlete hero worship, isolated leaderboards, sterile spreadsheets. |
| **Premium & Contemporary** | Generous whitespace, refined borders, sophisticated dark surfaces, editorial typography. | Cheap template design, generic AI startup gradients, excessive glassmorphism. |
| **Local & Authentic** | Real Noida sectors (Sector 21A, Sector 137, Expressway), actual park gates, local meeting spots. | Anonymous stock cities, generic palm trees, fake corporate testimonials. |
| **Slightly Futuristic** | High-precision micro-interactions, sleek badges, crisp status indicators, modern web standards. | Sci-fi gimmicks, cyberpunk tropes, distracting 3D models. |

## 2. Voice and Tone

Our voice is that of a **trusted, energetic local run captain who knows every trail, track, and coffee stop in the city**. We are knowledgeable, direct, welcoming, and grounded.

### 1. Direct & Human
We speak with concise, active phrasing. We respect the user's intelligence and time.
- **Do:** *"Tuesday Track Session at Noida Stadium. Meet at Gate 4 at 5:45 AM. All paces welcome."*
- **Don't:** *"Are you ready to unlock your maximum physical potential and crush your morning fitness goals? Join our tribe today!"*

### 2. Hyperlocal Grounding
We use the vernacular of Noida and Greater Noida naturally.
- **Do:** *"Long run route: Advant Navis to Sector 168 service road and back (16km)."*
- **Don't:** *"A scenic run along the local highway area."*

### 3. Inclusive & Encouraging (Without Being Preachy)
We normalize starting where you are while celebrating consistency.
- **Do:** *"Paced groups for 5:30/km, 6:30/km, and run-walk intervals."*
- **Don't:** *"No pain no gain. Only the strong survive."*

## 3. Copywriting Rules & Vocabulary

### Primary Action Copy (CTAs)

| Purpose | Approved Copy | Banned Generic Copy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Site CTA** | **Explore Noida Fitness** | Get Started / Learn More / Sign Up Now |
| **Community Directory** | **Find Your Community** | View Clubs / Directory / Browse |
| **Event Calendar** | **Explore This Week's Events** | See Events / Buy Tickets / Calendar |
| **Community Profile Action** | **Join Community** | Register / Subscribe / Follow Us |
| **Event Attendance** | **RSVP for Event** / **Save Spot** | Buy Now / Book Ticket / Convert |
| **Organizer Intake** | **List Your Community** | Partner With Us / For Enterprise |

### Banned Jargon Glossary

Never use the following cliché corporate fitness terms anywhere on NOIDA.FIT:
- ❌ *"Unlock your potential"*
- ❌ *"Transform your fitness journey"*
- ❌ *"Crush your limits"*
- ❌ *"Level up your grind"*
- ❌ *"Next-generation wellness ecosystem"*
- ❌ *"Gamify your workouts"*
- ❌ *"Synergistic community engagement"*
