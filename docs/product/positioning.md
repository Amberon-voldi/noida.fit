## 2. Positioning Statement

For **active and aspiring individuals living or working in Noida and Greater Noida** who want to find real-world training groups and fitness activities, **NOIDA.FIT** is the **city-first community platform** that connects people with local running clubs, fitness groups, training sessions, and wellness gatherings.

Unlike **Strava**, which prioritizes solitary telemetry and performance leaderboards, or **Cult.fit**, which restricts users to proprietary closed-door corporate gym classes, **NOIDA.FIT** is an **open cultural gateway** to the entire grassroots fitness movement of Noida—celebrating community, shared movement, and real-world participation.

---

## 3. Target Audiences

### Primary Users: The Active Urbanites
- **Demographics:** Ages 18–42; working professionals, university students, entrepreneurs, and active residents of Noida (Sectors 18 to 150), Greater Noida (West/Extension and Expressway sectors), and South/East Delhi commuters.
- **Mindset:** Values physical health, social connection, and city lifestyle. Wants to stay fit without feeling isolated in a dark basement gym.
- **Pain Points:**
  - Moves into a new apartment complex in Noida and doesn't know where runners or cyclists meet.
  - Wants to play badminton, tennis, or padel on weekends but lacks a regular group.
  - Finds WhatsApp groups chaotic, hidden, and hard to discover without a personal referral.
  - Misses out on community long runs or outdoor workouts because event announcements are scattered across Instagram stories.
- **Core Activities:**
  - Running (road, trail, park laps)
  - Strength & functional conditioning
  - Cycling (Noida-Greater Noida Expressway, service roads)
  - Racquet sports (Badminton, Tennis, Squash, Pickleball)
  - Outdoor bootcamps & calisthenics
  - Yoga, breathwork, and mobility sessions
  - Weekend social runs and post-workout coffee gatherings

### Secondary Users: Community Leaders & Fitness Creators
- **Organizers & Run Captains:** Volunteer captains running local running clubs (e.g. 50–500 members) needing a polished public link for their schedule instead of answering repeated DMs.
- **Independent Coaches & Personal Trainers:** Skilled local coaches hosting weekend bootcamps or track sessions seeking local visibility.
- **Independent Studios & Box Owners:** Boutique yoga, CrossFit, Pilates, and MMA studio owners wanting organic community discovery without spending exorbitant Meta ad budgets.
- **Local Fitness Brands & Venues:** Local cafes, recovery hubs, sports arenas, and hydration brands looking to support authentic local fitness events.

## 4. Scope Guardrails & Intentional Exclusions (V1)

To ensure laser focus and rapid execution, the following boundaries are strictly enforced for V1:

### 1. Broad Categories Only (No Niche Sport Specialization)
- **Constraint:** Do **NOT** build dedicated database schemas, specialized workflows, or custom UI dashboards tailored to specific branded sports formats (e.g., HYROX, CrossFit Open, Ironman).
- **Rule:** These sessions must live naturally under broad, standardized category concepts:
  - *Fitness Gathering*
  - *Community Run*
  - *Training Session*
  - *Group Workout*
  - *Sports Activity*
  - *Outdoor Bootcamp*

### 2. Not a Performance Tracker
- **Constraint:** Do **NOT** build Strava-like GPS route tracking, pacing splits, heart-rate monitors, or segment leaderboards.
- **Rule:** If a community uses Strava or Garmin, provide an external link on their community profile. NOIDA.FIT is the discovery and gathering layer, not the telemetry layer.

### 3. Not a Generic Gym Directory (No Justdial/Yellowpages Model)
- **Constraint:** Do **NOT** scrape thousands of dead business listings or cold gym contacts with no community activity.
- **Rule:** Places featured on NOIDA.FIT must have active communities, open tracks, or scheduled public sessions.

### 4. Not a Closed Corporate Gym Chain
- **Constraint:** NOIDA.FIT is platform-agnostic. It does not sell private gym memberships or restrict access to single branded facilities.
